\textbf{Application for the Dance Intensive Program}

\bigskip

Salut!

My name is Étienne and I am a dancer. It took a lot for me to proudly say that.

At six years old, I fell in love with dance. It got me cast aside in my school and angered my father.
Going to middle school, I quit dance to try and make friends and appease my dad.

Eight years later, I found the courage to dance again, and met Maïlys.
She saw potential in me, and gave me a platform to dance and create together.
We collaborated/created for three years.

After moving away, it was difficult for me to find a similar supportive environment.
I danced a bit but had to confront my daemons in more ways than one.
I lived and recovered from substance abuse, went through therapy, and learned to love who I am.

During these years I still got to work with some beautiful dancing souls like Eva.

I have also been writing software for fifteen years, professionally for ten,
but recently quit, as being a software employee is not fulfilling me.

That does not mean that I am leaving software behind entirely.
I am still doing some coding most days,
and in the future I see myself freelancing software to finance my life and artistic endeavors.

I feel present, and ready to give dance its rightful place in my life.

Tanzfabrik's Dance Intensive is the perfect way for me to do so, and commit.

There is so much that I don't know, so much to find, to feel, to explore and
create that I am hoping to advance on within the framework of your school.
