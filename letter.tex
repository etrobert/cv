\textbf{Application for the Dance Intensive Program}

\bigskip

Salut!

My name is Étienne and I am a dancer.

It took a lot for me to be able to proudly say that:

2002: I fall in love with dance.
I am a social pariah at school.
My father is ashamed of me.

2006: I quit the thing I love most to help make friends and appease my dad.

2014 - 2016: I find the courage to dance again.
I meet Maïlys.
She sees potential in me, and offers to research and compose together.
For three years, we will be working together.

2016 - 2025: I move away from Bordeaux.
I am searching for dance as I know it from Maïlys but cannot come close.
I am building a career in software.
I have beautiful interactions, cross the path fo beautiful souls.
I stay at cautious distance.
I dance at TanzFabrik (from 2020) and enjoy the environment there.
I go through therapy, learn to confront my daemons and love myself.
